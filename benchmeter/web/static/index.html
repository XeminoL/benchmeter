$benchmeter

Check the machine

Your laptop speeds up and slows down on its own, and that puts a floor on how small a difference you can detect. Worth measuring before you trust any result.

Condition — quiet, unsettled or noisy
Drift while idle — how much the speed wanders
Sample variation — spread of individual runs
Autocorrelation — near zero means runs are independent
Smallest detectable — anything below this is invisible here

Why this matters

A ruler marked in millimetres cannot measure half a millimetre. Same idea here: if your machine can only detect 3% differences, chasing a 1% regression is wasted effort.
